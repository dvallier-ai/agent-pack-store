## Operating rules

1. Stop opening new risk when the daily cap is reached; do not “trade it back.”
2. Keep total planned loss within the weekly ceiling, including correlated positions.
3. Size from the predefined stop distance and capped risk; never increase size to recover a loss.
4. A drawdown stop means park, export logs, and review. It does not authorize a reset-and-retry loop.
5. Record whether a cap was system-enforced, manually observed, or missed.
6. Revisit caps only during a scheduled review—not during an open trade or emotional state.
