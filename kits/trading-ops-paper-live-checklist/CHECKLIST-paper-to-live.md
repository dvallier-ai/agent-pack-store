# Paper→Live Checklist

Use this as a gate, not a prediction tool. Complete each line with evidence or mark it **NO**.

## 1. Paper hygiene

- [ ] Account and environment are clearly labeled **PAPER** in screenshots, logs, and filenames.
- [ ] Strategy version, market, timeframe, fees/slippage assumptions, and data window are recorded.
- [ ] Every order has a timestamp, side, size, entry, stop, exit/expiry, and reason.
- [ ] Rejected, missed, partial, and manually changed orders are logged—not just winners.
- [ ] Paper fills are not treated as equivalent to live fills or liquidity.
- [ ] The Paper3 Alpaca artifacts are stored as proof of process only; no paper PnL is described as income.

## 2. Risk caps

- [ ] A hard daily loss cap is written in dollars and as a percentage.
- [ ] A weekly account-risk ceiling is written and does not exceed the chosen limit.
- [ ] Per-trade risk, position-size ceiling, and open-position count are defined.
- [ ] Stop placement is decided before entry; no averaging down or martingale sizing.
- [ ] Fees, spread, slippage, gaps, and outages have a written handling rule.

## 3. Kill rules

- [ ] Drawdown stop and review threshold are explicit.
- [ ] Two or more process breaks trigger a pause and review.
- [ ] Revenge-trading or emotional override triggers an immediate park.
- [ ] A recovery procedure says who/what can restart the system and when.

## 4. Journal quality

- [ ] Each paper session has a completed journal, including no-trade sessions.
- [ ] Thesis, invalidation, size, stop, outcome, and lesson are captured.
- [ ] Execution quality is separated from outcome quality.
- [ ] The review checks rule-following, not only PnL.

## 5. Go / no-go review

Mark one: **GO FOR FURTHER REVIEW / NO-GO / STAY PAPER**

- [ ] Evidence covers a pre-defined sample and multiple market conditions.
- [ ] The operator can follow every rule without improvising.
- [ ] Caps and kill rules are technically enforceable or monitored.
- [ ] A small, predefined live test—if ever chosen—is affordable to lose and separately approved.
- [ ] Current evidence, account permissions, tax/legal context, and operational risks have been independently checked.

**Decision:** ____________________  **Date:** __________  **Reviewer:** ____________________

A “GO” here is not a trade instruction or a forecast. It only means the process is ready for an independent decision about what to test next.
