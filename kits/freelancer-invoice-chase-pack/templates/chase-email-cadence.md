# Chase email cadence (warm clients)

**Tone:** calm, specific, easy to pay. No threats. No fake “final notice” theater.  
**Schedule from invoice send date (Day 0).** Adjust if your contract says otherwise.

Replace bracketed fields. Attach or link the invoice every time.

---

## Day 0 — Invoice send

**Subject:** Invoice INV-YYYYMMDD-001 · [Project] · due [Due date]

Hi [Name],

Attached is invoice **INV-YYYYMMDD-001** for **[short deliverable summary]** ($Z).

- **Due:** [Due date] ([Net-15/Net-30])
- **Pay:** [ACH / Wise / link — one clear method]
- **Memo:** INV-YYYYMMDD-001

Happy to answer any AP questions. Thanks!

[Your name]

---

## Day +7 — Friendly nudge (if unpaid)

**Subject:** Re: Invoice INV-YYYYMMDD-001 · quick check-in

Hi [Name],

Friendly bump on **INV-YYYYMMDD-001** ($Z), due [Due date].

If it’s already in the payment run, ignore this. If you need a different format (PO, W-9, PDF), tell me what AP needs and I’ll send it today.

Thanks,  
[Your name]

---

## Day +14 — Clarify blocker

**Subject:** INV-YYYYMMDD-001 · anything blocking payment?

Hi [Name],

Checking in on **INV-YYYYMMDD-001** ($Z), now [X] days past due / due [Due date].

Is there a blocker on your side (PO, approval, different payee details)? I’ll fix whatever’s in my court within one business day.

Payment details again: [one method + memo INV-…]

Thanks,  
[Your name]

---

## Day +21 — Direct ask + next step

**Subject:** INV-YYYYMMDD-001 · please confirm payment date

Hi [Name],

I still show **INV-YYYYMMDD-001** ($Z) unpaid. Can you confirm a **payment date** this week, or loop in whoever owns AP?

If scope or acceptance is the issue, say so — we can resolve that separately from payment timing.

Thanks for sorting this,  
[Your name]

---

## Optional Day +30 — Escalate politely (contract-aware)

Only if your agreement allows and the relationship supports it. Still no threats.

**Subject:** INV-YYYYMMDD-001 · outstanding balance

Hi [Name],

**INV-YYYYMMDD-001** ($Z) remains open as of [today]. Please send payment by [firm date] or reply with the scheduled pay date from AP.

If you prefer a short call to close this out, I’m available [windows].

[Your name]

---

## Cadence tips

- One channel primary (email); SMS only if they already text you
- Log send dates next to the invoice number
- Never invent late fees that aren’t in the signed agreement
- Pair month-end with Accounting Micro checklist so you know what’s still open
