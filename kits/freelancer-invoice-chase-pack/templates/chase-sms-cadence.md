# Chase SMS / iMessage cadence

**Use only with clients who already text you.** Not for cold numbers. Keep under ~160–300 chars. No threats.

---

## Day +3 (optional soft ping)

Hi [Name] — sent INV-YYYYMMDD-001 ($Z) by email for [project], due [date]. Want me to resend PDF or drop pay details here?

## Day +10

Quick bump: INV-YYYYMMDD-001 still open on my side. Already in AP, or need a different format?

## Day +18

Still showing INV-YYYYMMDD-001 unpaid. Can you share expected pay date or AP contact? Happy to fix blockers same day.

## Stop rules

- If they say “paid” → ask for confirmation (last4 / txid / screenshot) once, then thank them
- If they ask to stop SMS → move to email only
- Never text outside reasonable hours in *their* timezone
