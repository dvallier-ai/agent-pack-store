# Payment terms blurbs (paste into proposals)

Pick one. Align with your signed SOW — these are **templates**, not legal advice.

## Net-15 (default for small scopes)

Invoices are due within **15 days** of issue date (Net-15). Work product is delivered per the SOW; payment is due as invoiced. Please include the invoice number in the payment memo.

## Net-30 (larger clients / AP cycles)

Invoices are due within **30 days** of issue date (Net-30). If your AP needs a PO, W-9, or vendor packet, request it before kickoff so Day-0 invoicing isn’t blocked.

## Deposit + remainder

**50%** due on acceptance of this proposal to reserve the start date; **50%** due on delivery / acceptance of [milestone]. Late start may slip if the deposit is delayed.

## Kill fee (optional)

If the client cancels after kickoff for convenience, fees for work completed through the cancel date are due within Net-15, plus any non-refundable third-party costs already approved in writing.

## What not to invent

- “Collections will begin” threats
- Late fees not present in the signed agreement
- Claiming attorney review you didn’t get
