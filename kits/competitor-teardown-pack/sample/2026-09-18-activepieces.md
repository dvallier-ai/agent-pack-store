## 1. One-line positioning
Activepieces sells **all-in-one AI automation** with a visual builder, TypeScript “pieces,” MCP tooling, and a **true MIT community core** you can self-host without a credit meter — then upsells cloud credits / team governance.

## 2. Who it’s for
| Segment | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Technical indie / SMB | **High** | Self-host CE = unlimited runs; cloud Free to explore |
| Non-technical solo | **Medium** | Builder is approachable; self-host still needs ops skill |
| Enterprise IT | **Rising** | Team/Ultimate: SSO, SCIM, audit, Git Sync, vaults |
| Embed / ISV | **Niche** | White-label “Embed” from ~$36k/yr |

## 3. Pricing snapshot (public, checked 2026-09-18)
From [activepieces.com/pricing](https://www.activepieces.com/pricing):

| Plan | List (monthly toggle) | Credits / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 100 credits/day; flows + agents + tables + MCP/API |
| **Plus** | **$20/mo** | 10,000 credits/mo; ≤5 users; BYO AI keys; overage **$0.007**/credit |
| **Team** | **$200/mo** | 50,000 credits/mo; 25 users; SSO; projects; email support |
| **Ultimate** | Custom | Yearly credit pool; SCIM; Git Sync; audit; dedicated support |
| **Embed** | From **~$36k/yr** | White-label builder + SDK |
| **Community Edition (self-host)** | Free MIT core | Unlimited runs/users/flows; **no** Agents/Chat, Projects, API, SSO, audit, Git Sync, etc. (per pricing FAQ) |

Annual billing on third-party writeups often cites Plus ~$16/mo and Team ~$166/mo ([noderidge review, Sep 2026 check](https://noderidge.com/activepieces-review-small-business-automation-2026/)) — always re-verify the live pricing page.

**Billing model:** one standard flow run ≈ **1 credit** regardless of step count; AI steps cost more unless BYO key (Plus+).

## 4. Product surface (public claims)
- Visual flows: loops, branches, retries, HTTP, JS/TS code ([GitHub README](https://github.com/activepieces/activepieces))
- **280–760+** pieces / integrations (README vs pricing “760+” embed copy — count moves; cite directory: [activepieces.com/pieces](https://www.activepieces.com/pieces))
- AI agents, chat-to-automation, tables, **MCP** exposure of pieces
- Open ecosystem: pieces as npm packages; community contribution emphasized
- License split: **MIT core** + separately licensed enterprise directory ([solutions/open-source-developers](https://www.activepieces.com/solutions/open-source-developers))

## 5. Competitive frame
| vs | Activepieces edge | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Zapier** | Cheaper at volume; self-host; open pieces | Smaller app catalog / brand trust; less “set and forget” for non-tech |
| **Make** | OSS path; credit-per-run simplicity | Make still stronger for deep visual scenarios / agency templates |
| **n8n** | MIT (clearer OSS story than n8n’s fair-code SUL); polished cloud Free | n8n has deeper community mindshare + node depth for power users |

## 6. Positioning gaps (judgment overlay — paid brief value)
1. **CE vs cloud confusion:** Buyers hear “unlimited free self-host” then discover Agents/Chat/API/SSO sit behind paid — sales motion must pre-empt “bait” objections (FAQ already does; collateral often doesn’t).
2. **Credit + AI surcharge literacy:** “1 credit per run” is a win vs Zapier tasks — until AI steps burn 2–20 credits; BYO keys are the real Plus upsell.
3. **Integration trust:** Piece count is high, but SMB buyers care about *their* stack (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro) — vertical landing pages are thin vs Zapier.
4. **Governance cliff:** Free/Plus → Team ($200) is a steep jump for a 6-person shop that only needs SSO.

- Indie SaaS founder evaluating **build-vs-buy** automation for onboarding
- Agency deciding whether to standardize on AP vs n8n for client ops
- Local-services “techie nephew” comparing self-host cost to Zapier Professional
