## What the client sends (redacted)

Ask clients to send **only** what is needed. Prefer screenshots / PDF exports with account numbers masked.

### Required
- [ ] Business one-liner (what they sell) + tools used (QBO / QB Desktop / other)
- [ ] Month(s) they care about cleaning: _______________
- [ ] Screenshot or PDF: **Chart of Accounts** (account names visible; balances optional)
- [ ] Screenshot or PDF: **Bank register / For Review** queue (recent uncleared / uncategorized)
- [ ] Screenshot or PDF: **Profit & Loss** for last full month (or YTD) — amounts OK; strip SSN/EIN
- [ ] List of known messy areas in their own words (e.g. “Stripe deposits double,” “personal Amazon”)

### Optional (helps prioritization)
- [ ] Balance Sheet snapshot
- [ ] A/R aging screenshot
- [ ] Sales-tax center / filing reminder screen (**no advice requested** — context only)
- [ ] How many bank + CC accounts connected: ____

### Redaction rules (client instructions — paste into your order form)
1. Mask full bank account and card numbers (show last 4 only if needed).  
2. Remove SSN / EIN / home address.  
3. Blur customer names/emails on invoices if not needed for the question.  
4. Do **not** send live bank login credentials, QB password, or MFA codes.  
5. Prefer export screenshots over sharing full company-file access for this SKU.

## What you deliver (operator checklist)

Return a short written artifact (PDF or markdown), e.g.:

1. **Top 5–10 prioritized cleanup items** (P0 / P1 / P2)  
2. For each: *observation* (from their screenshots) → *suggested DIY action* → *risk if ignored* (ops risk, not legal conclusions)  
3. **Out of scope** list (tax positions, payroll, multi-entity, anything needing a licensed pro)  
4. **Suggested order of operations** for the next 7 days of DIY cleanup  
5. Repeat the disclaimer footer on every deliverable

### Example priority line (tone)

> **P0 — Processor deposits:** P&L shows both gross sales and Stripe payout deposits categorized as income. DIY: map payouts to clearing/liability or match to payments; stop double-counting. Verify with your processor reports. *Not tax advice.*

## Operator boundaries (do not cross)

- Do not file taxes, sign forms, or call agencies on their behalf under this SKU  
- Do not invent deductions or say “this is deductible”  
- Do not request full QB admin password for a $49–$99 async list (if you later sell access-based cleanup, use a separate higher-touch offer with clearer scope)  
- If the file looks like audit / notice / multi-state nexus complexity → refer to a licensed CPA/EA and stop
